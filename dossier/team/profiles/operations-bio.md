TITLE: Marta Reyes — Operations Lead
STATUS: APPROVED

Marta Reyes runs operations at Sable Quay Advisory. She joined in 2024 after a decade in professional services operations, most recently as practice manager for a regional consulting firm.

Marta owns the parts of the studio that make the work dependable: engagement scheduling, associate contracting, quality review, invoicing and the document library. She also runs the client onboarding process and makes sure every engagement letter is signed and properly scoped before work begins.

She is the first point of contact for new clients and for anything to do with scheduling, billing or contracting. If you are unsure who to ask, ask Marta.

Marta holds a degree in business administration and a professional qualification in project management. She works from the studio's office in Bristol, usually four days a week, and coordinates the associate network remotely.
