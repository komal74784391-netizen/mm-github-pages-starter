TITLE: Dana Whitlock — Principal
STATUS: APPROVED

Dana Whitlock is the principal and founder of Sable Quay Advisory. She has spent eighteen years on commercial strategy and pricing questions, first in industry and then as an independent advisor.

Before founding Sable Quay, Dana led the commercial strategy function at a mid-market industrial group, where she rebuilt discount governance across four business units. She has since advised owner-led businesses in retail, logistics, business services and specialised manufacturing.

Dana's work focuses on the moment when a company's pricing or commercial model stops matching how it actually sells. She is known for short, direct diagnostics and for writing recommendations that a leadership team can act on without a further study.

She holds an MBA from a European business school and a first degree in economics. She writes occasionally on pricing governance and teaches a short course on commercial decision-making for owner-managers. Dana works from the studio's office in Bristol and travels for client workshops.
