TITLE: Market Note: Pricing Pressure in Mid-Market Consulting (2026)
STATUS: APPROVED
AUTHOR: Dana Whitlock
PUBLISHED: 2026-10-08

Mid-market consulting is under visible pricing pressure in 2026. Three patterns are worth watching.

First, buyers are consolidating suppliers. Where a company once held four or five specialist relationships, it is now looking for two or three firms that can cover more ground. Specialist studios still win on depth, but they are being asked to justify why they should sit alongside a larger firm rather than replace it.

Second, day-rate benchmarks are getting harder to defend. Procurement teams increasingly compare rates across categories, not within them, and a principal rate that looks reasonable in one category can look high in another. The practical response is to price against the decision being made, not against a market average.

Third, fixed-fee and outcome-linked arrangements are becoming more common, but they are not a cure for margin pressure. Clients accept them when the scope is genuinely bounded; they resist them when the fee looks like a day-rate calculation in disguise.

Our own view is that the studios that hold their pricing through this period will be those that can describe their scope precisely and walk away from work that does not fit it.
