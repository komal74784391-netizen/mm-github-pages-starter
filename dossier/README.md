Sable Quay Advisory is an independent research and advisory studio working with mid-market companies on pricing, commercial strategy and operating-model questions. We are deliberately small: a principal, an operations lead, and a network of specialist associates brought in for individual engagements.

We work in short, focused cycles. Most engagements begin with a two-week diagnostic, produce a decision-ready set of options, and then move into an implementation sprint only if the client chooses to continue. We do not run long open-ended retainers.

Our clients are typically owner-led businesses between 40 and 400 people, or PE-backed platforms that need an independent read on a commercial question before a transaction. We are comfortable working alongside in-house finance and commercial teams, and we write for the people who have to make the decision rather than for an academic audience.

Everything we publish is drawn from client work that has been reviewed and cleared for external use. Where a client prefers not to be named, we describe the engagement by sector and scale only.
