TITLE: Engagement Model
STATUS: APPROVED
AUTHOR: Dana Whitlock

Every Sable Quay engagement runs through four stages.

Scope. A one-page engagement letter fixes the question, the decision the client needs to make, and the boundary of the work. If we cannot state the decision in one sentence, we do not start.

Diagnose. The team gathers data, interviews the people closest to the work, and builds a quantified picture. We share early findings in a working session rather than saving everything for a final presentation.

Options. We present two or three genuinely different options, each with costs, risks and what would have to be true for it to work. We include a recommendation, but the client makes the decision.

Support. If the client chooses to proceed, we support implementation in short cycles. We do not run the implementation for the client; we work alongside the team that owns it.

Staffing is deliberately lean. A principal leads every engagement, one senior associate runs the day-to-day work, and analysts are added only where the data volume requires it. Associates are introduced by name before the engagement begins, and clients can veto any team member.
