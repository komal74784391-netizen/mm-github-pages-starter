TITLE: 2026–27 Rate Card — Approved
STATUS: APPROVED — CURRENT VERSION
ISSUED: 2026-10-01
APPROVED BY: Dana Whitlock

This rate card supersedes all earlier versions and applies from 1 October 2026 through 30 September 2027.

Standard rates (per consultant day):
- Principal: 2,400
- Senior associate: 1,750
- Analyst: 1,050

Engagement minimums:
- Commercial diagnostic: 12 consultant days.
- Pricing and packaging redesign: 18 consultant days.
- Operating-model review: 25 consultant days.

Expenses are billed at cost with a 5% administration charge. Travel is quoted separately and requires written approval before booking.

Payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. A 2% early-settlement discount applies to invoices settled within 10 days. Rates for multi-track programmes are not discounted; instead, we offer a fixed-fee option where the scope is well bounded, priced at 110% of the estimated day-rate total.

Associates are billed at their agreed individual rates, disclosed in the engagement letter. Nothing in this rate card overrides a signed engagement letter.
