TITLE: Advisory Services — Overview
STATUS: APPROVED
AUTHOR: Dana Whitlock

Sable Quay works across three advisory tracks.

1. Commercial diagnostics. A structured review of pricing, discounting, sales coverage and retention economics. Typically four to six weeks, ending with a quantified view of where margin is leaking and a short list of changes worth making.

2. Pricing and packaging redesign. We rebuild price architecture, discount governance and packaging for products or service lines that have outgrown their original model. This track usually follows a diagnostic, but clients sometimes bring us in directly when a major launch or renewal cycle is already underway.

3. Operating-model reviews. For businesses whose growth has outpaced their internal structure, we map decision rights, hand-offs and reporting lines, then propose a lighter operating cadence. These reviews are usually eight to twelve weeks and involve working sessions with the leadership team.

Each track can be run independently. We deliberately avoid bundling all three into a single large programme, because most clients get more value from doing one thing properly and then deciding what comes next.
