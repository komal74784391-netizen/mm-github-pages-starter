TITLE: Case Study: Regional Logistics Network
STATUS: APPROVED
CLIENT: a regional logistics operator, 11 depots (name withheld)

A regional logistics operator had added depots quickly to win a large retail contract. Service levels improved, but cost per parcel rose sharply and the depot network no longer matched where volumes actually sat.

We spent four weeks building a volume and cost map by depot, lane and customer, then tested three network options: consolidate, rebalance, and re-contract. Each option was costed at the same volume assumptions and stress-tested against the loss of the anchor contract.

The client chose a rebalanced network: two depots closed, one expanded, and trunk routes redrawn. Implementation took two quarters and was run by the client's operations team with our support. Cost per parcel fell 14% while on-time delivery held steady.

The work depended on clean data from the client's transport management system. Where records were incomplete, we reconstructed volumes from customer invoices and reconciled them with the finance team before using them in the model.
