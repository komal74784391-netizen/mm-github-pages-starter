TITLE: Case Study: Coastal Retail Turnaround
STATUS: APPROVED
CLIENT: a regional home and garden retailer, 62 stores (name withheld)

The client had grown through acquisitions and found that gross margin had fallen by four points over two years while sales were flat. Store managers were discounting heavily at month end, and the promotional calendar had no owner.

Sable Quay ran a six-week commercial diagnostic across the store estate and the central buying team. We rebuilt the promotional calendar around a small number of planned events, introduced a discount authority matrix with clear limits by role, and moved month-end reporting from a sales-volume target to a margin target.

The result was a 3.1 point recovery in gross margin over the following two quarters, achieved without reducing like-for-like sales. The discount authority matrix remained in use after the engagement and was later adopted by the client's trade division.

The engagement used two consultants at peak. The client's commercial director described the work as "the first time we could see where the money was actually going".
