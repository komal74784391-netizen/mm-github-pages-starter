TITLE: Client FAQ
STATUS: APPROVED

How do we start an engagement?
Send a short note describing the decision you need to make and the timing. We will usually reply within two working days to suggest a call.

What does a first conversation involve?
A 30-minute call with the principal, at no cost. We use it to understand the question and to decide whether we are the right firm for it. If we are not, we will say so and suggest alternatives.

How quickly can you start?
Typically two to four weeks from a signed engagement letter, depending on the team required. Shorter starts are sometimes possible for diagnostics.

Do you work fixed fee?
Yes, where the scope is well bounded. Otherwise we work on a day-rate basis with an agreed minimum.

What do you need from us?
Access to the relevant data, a small number of interviews, and a decision owner who can attend the working sessions. We do not need a full project management office.

Who owns the work product?
The client owns everything we produce. We ask for permission before referencing an engagement externally, and we never publish client names without written approval.

How do you handle billing?
Invoices are issued monthly in arrears against the engagement letter. Standard terms are 30 days. Marta Reyes handles all billing questions.
